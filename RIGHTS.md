# Payload rights and publication scope

Only newly authored accounting code, synthetic fixtures/sensitivities, original analysis, source URLs/commit IDs/hashes, and selected factual report aggregates are included. No upstream source files, report prose copies, strategy configuration files, operational scripts, account identifiers, wallets, keys, customer records or raw market datasets are included. The user approved publication of this reviewed package and MIT licensing for the newly authored code.

No top-level source license was found for mm-bot-new at the inspected commits. Public accessibility does not establish a right to redistribute its source code. Third-party licenses under the source repository's node_modules do not license the bot. Consequently, this package links to source rather than copying it. No third-party library is bundled; the accounting utility uses Python's standard library. The MIT license in LICENSE applies only to fee_adjust.py and tests/test_fees.py. Documentation, factual aggregate files and sensitivity data are outside that license grant; no upstream source or third-party dataset rights are granted.

The selected numerical facts in data/reported_aggregates.csv and the original summary are attributed to a publicly accessible user-directed report. Raw Binance/Phoenix datasets were not obtained or redistributed; their redistribution rights have not been verified. Any future raw-data publication requires source terms and authorization review, sanitization, and manifest updates. Dataset absence also prevents reconstruction of historical runs.
